Methodology and scope

Collection covers ransomware leak sites reached over Tor, cybercrime forums and markets, breach and infostealer credential sources, paste sites, and certificate transparency logs. Matching uses the watch terms and business profile configured for {{ client.name }}. Initial access broker candidates are matched on business profile rather than name and are leads for confirmation. Vendetta collects passively and does not purchase, download data sets, or interact with threat actors. This report reflects information available at the time of collection and does not constitute a guarantee that all exposure has been identified.
