Sources

Onion sources are seeded daily from the tracked group lists. A profile (a file in app/profiles) turns a plain crawl into per-post or per-thread parsing. For a board your team has an account on, paste the session cookie from an authenticated browser; it is sent read-only and never used to log in or post. Sites that fail five times are retried every 12 hours.
