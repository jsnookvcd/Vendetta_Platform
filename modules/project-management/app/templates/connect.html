Run your boards, tasks, meeting notes and Harvest time from Claude. Claude acts as you, with your access level; a viewer gets the read-only tools.

Claude (web, desktop, mobile)

  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and add a custom connector.
  2. Name it Vendetta PM and paste this server URL:
    {{ mcp_url }}
  3. Leave the OAuth client fields empty. Claude registers itself.
  4. Click Connect. You sign in here with Microsoft 365 and approve access once.
  5. In a chat, turn the connector on and ask: "what's on my plate", "log 2 hours to VCD-IR-2026-445 forensic analysis yesterday", "write up this call on the Rize engagement and add the action items".

An organization owner may need to add the connector for the whole Team or Enterprise workspace first.
